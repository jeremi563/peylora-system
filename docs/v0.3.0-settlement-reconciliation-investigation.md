# v0.3.0 Settlement Reconciliation Investigation

**Status:** Investigation only; no implementation scope approved yet

**Baseline:** `main` at `0d8f0b7bdd8ba3c6c75a0a59a06c30e4e66d13a9` (`v0.2.0`)

## Agreed first-round decisions

- v0.3.0 is settlement reconciliation only; actual refunds remain deferred.
- MVP evidence sources are CSV, Excel and manual bank-deposit entry.
- A settlement is not authority-confirmed from provider payment evidence alone: the reconciliation workflow requires both provider settlement evidence and bank/deposit evidence.
- Auto-confirmation is limited to exact provider receipt/reference matches with consistent currency and amount/date evidence. Ambiguous candidates remain exceptions.
- Operator resolution is record-only: operators may confirm, reject or explain reconciliation relationships, but may not mutate Provider Transactions, allocations, Customer Credit or unassigned funds.
- CSV and Excel imports use operator-managed source profiles only; unknown or changed headers remain validation exceptions.
- Canonical settlement data retains both batches and individual lines.
- Gross, fee/deduction and net values remain source fields; Peylora does not infer missing fee formulas unless the source profile explicitly defines them.
- Provider transaction date, settlement/effective date and bank deposit/value date remain separate.
- Merchants register bank account aliases/references for manual deposit and statement validation.

## Agreed final-round decisions

- Canonical settlement statuses are `EXPECTED`, `PARTIAL`, `SETTLED`, `DELAYED`, `ADJUSTED`, `CONFLICT` and `UNKNOWN`.
- Reversal and adjustment rows are preserved as append-only evidence; actual refund processing remains deferred.
- Completed reconciliation runs create immutable snapshots; live derived state remains queryable.
- Original settlement evidence is retained for the Merchant's financial-history lifetime.
- A settlement line with no Provider Transaction remains an exception; Peylora never fabricates a Provider Transaction.

## 1. Executive summary

Peylora should treat settlement reconciliation as a separate financial process. It must reconcile authoritative merchant settlement evidence against Provider Transactions, received funds, and bank deposits without rewriting Payment Attempt or Provider Transaction outcomes.

The official Safaricom Daraja portal is verified to provide access to Safaricom and M-Pesa APIs for web and mobile payment integration. The portal evidence available to this investigation does not establish a public, documented settlement-payout API or document a settlement batch schema. Existing Daraja capabilities relevant here are provider transaction status, account balance and M-Pesa transaction operations, but they do not prove that a merchant has received a bank settlement.

The realistic v0.3.0 architecture is therefore:

1. Preserve immutable provider confirmation evidence.
2. Add merchant-scoped settlement evidence ingestion.
3. Parse and retain the original source as append-only evidence.
4. Match settlement lines to Provider Transactions and settlement batches to bank deposits.
5. Record matches, exceptions and operator resolutions without changing received amounts or allocations.
6. Keep refunds, reversals and settlement corrections in separate workflows until their provider and business rules are documented.

CSV or downloadable merchant statements are the most credible first ingestion target. A direct provider API should be treated as a future adapter only if an official, authenticated Safaricom settlement/report API is documented and authorised for the merchant.

## 3. Evidence-access limitations

Official Safaricom pages are partly authentication-gated or bot-gated. Their public pages did not expose a verified settlement-batch API or downloadable schema during this investigation.

Peylora must not claim:

- That STK success proves settlement.
- That an account-balance response proves a specific payout.
- That a portal statement is automatically available to every merchant.
- That Daraja exposes a settlement API merely because it exposes payment APIs.
- That bank references, settlement dates, gross/net amounts or transaction counts have one universal meaning across all merchant products.

The merchant should remain responsible for identifying and authorising the evidence source. Safaricom may require different report products or account types for Paybill, Till and app-based settlement arrangements.

## 4. Settlement data model requirements

### Canonical concepts

#### Settlement

A provider or banking arrangement's record that collected funds are due or available to a Merchant.

Settlement is distinct from:

- Provider Transaction: an individual confirmed provider payment.
- Payment Allocation: use of received funds against an Invoice or Customer Credit.
- Bank Deposit: movement of funds to the Merchant's bank account.
- Adjustment/Reversal: correction or reduction to a provider transaction or settlement.

#### Settlement Batch

A provider-created or provider-stated aggregation containing gross transaction value, fees/deductions, net payout, transaction count and settlement or payout dates.

The batch identifier is optional until a source proves that it exists. A source-period and source-row fingerprint must still identify the evidence.

#### Settlement Line

An individual transaction or adjustment reported by the settlement source.

A line should retain:

- Merchant ID.
- Source evidence ID.
- Source line number or row key.
- Provider transaction reference/receipt if supplied.
- Gross amount.
- Fee/deduction amount if supplied.
- Net amount if supplied.
- Transaction/capture date.
- Settlement/effective date if supplied.
- Currency.
- Line status.
- Original raw fields.

#### Settlement Deposit

A bank or settlement-account movement associated with a batch or group of batches.

It should retain bank deposit reference, deposit date, deposited amount, account identifier/reference and source evidence.

#### Settlement Match

An append-only association linking:

- Settlement line to Provider Transaction.
- Settlement batch to settlement line/deposit.
- Provider Transaction to allocation/Invoice/Customer Credit for context only.

A match must distinguish `SYSTEM_EXACT`, `SYSTEM_CANDIDATE`, `OPERATOR_CONFIRMED` and `CONFLICT`.

#### Reconciliation Exception

A durable discrepancy such as missing provider transaction, missing settlement line, duplicate row, amount mismatch, fee mismatch, partial settlement or conflicting source evidence.

#### Settlement Evidence

An immutable source envelope containing the raw file/API response, SHA-256 fingerprint, source profile, ingestion status, uploader/adapter, timestamps and merchant scope.

### Recommended table families

- `settlement_sources`
- `settlement_evidence_imports`
- `settlement_batches`
- `settlement_lines`
- `settlement_adjustments`
- `settlement_deposits`
- `settlement_matches`
- `settlement_exceptions`
- `settlement_exception_resolutions`

The exact column set belongs to the implementation ADR, but every financial table needs merchant scope and composite merchant keys where provider/settlement foreign keys are used.

## 5. Proposed reconciliation architecture

### State model

Settlement evidence should use explicit states:

- `RECEIVED`
- `VALIDATING`
- `VALIDATED`
- `MATCHING`
- `MATCHED`
- `PARTIALLY_MATCHED`
- `EXCEPTION`
- `SUPERSEDED`
- `ARCHIVED`

No state should imply that money has been credited to a bank account unless the source and/or bank evidence explicitly establishes it.

### Proposed flow

```text
Upload/API payload
  -> immutable evidence envelope
  -> source-profile validation
  -> canonical settlement batch/lines
  -> exact/candidate match engine
  -> append-only matches
  -> exceptions for discrepancies
  -> merchant/operator review
  -> audited resolution
  -> read-only reconciliation snapshot
```

### Matching rules

1. **Exact provider reference**
   - Match a settlement line to `provider_transactions.provider_reference` for a scoped merchant.
   - Require currency and amount consistency where both are authoritative.
2. **Receipt plus transaction date**
   - Receipt is a strong candidate match when no direct provider-transaction reference exists.
   - Verify merchant scope and date/amount before auto-confirmation.
3. **Amount, transaction date and source phone**
   - Create candidate matches only; never auto-confirm an ambiguous group.
4. **Settlement batch to deposit**
   - Match by bank reference if authoritative.
   - Otherwise compare aggregate net payout and deposit date/window.
   - Aggregate matching is never proof of a specific transaction's settlement.
5. **Fees/deductions**
   - Match from source rows if source provides them.
   - Do not derive fees silently from gross minus net unless the source schema explicitly guarantees that formula.
6. **Missing and duplicate records**
   - Missing provider transaction: settlement line exists with no candidate.
   - Missing settlement line: Provider Transaction exists with no settlement evidence.
   - Duplicate: same source fingerprint or natural identity is idempotent; materially different rows are exceptions.

Matching must not mutate:

- Provider Transaction outcome.
- Received amount.
- Payment Request fulfillment.
- Invoice allocation.
- Customer Credit.
- Unassigned received funds.

Those remain the existing financial ledger and require separate authorisation if corrected.

## 6. Proposed ingestion architecture

### MVP ingestion

Recommended first ingestion:

- Merchant-uploaded CSV.
- Merchant-uploaded Excel.
- Optional manual bank deposit entry.
- Append-only import staging.
- Explicit source-profile selection, for example `SAFARICOM_CSV_V1`.

PDF OCR should not be the first automated authority. If it is used, it should preserve the PDF and OCR text plus layout confidence, then require review or manual confirmation.

### Future provider/API adapter

A provider adapter interface can fetch, authenticate and normalise settlement evidence, but only after a documented official Safaricom settlement/report API is verified. An adapter must:

- Use merchant credentials or an authorised provider connection.
- Persist raw provider responses.
- Use pagination/cursor and idempotency safely.
- Reconcile source timestamps and periods.
- Not fabricate line identifiers or settlement totals.

### Import pipeline

1. Authenticate merchant or ADMIN import role.
2. Validate file type and size.
3. Compute SHA-256 fingerprint.
4. Store immutable original object.
5. Create import record in `RECEIVED`.
6. Validate required headers and source profile.
7. Parse into staging rows.
8. Reconcile duplicate source fingerprints/natural keys.
9. Canonicalise currencies and decimal precision.
10. Run matching engine.
11. Create matches and exceptions.
12. Publish an immutable reconciliation snapshot.
13. Audit every transition.

### Security

- Object storage should use private, merchant-scoped keys.
- Reject executable, HTML and unexpected MIME types.
- Limit decompression, row count and file size.
- Parse with strict encodings and spreadsheet formula protection.
- Retain original bytes and parsed source values.
- Never include uploaded evidence in public URLs or logs.
- Sign and expire download URLs.
- Restrict direct evidence downloads to Merchant ownership or ADMIN.

## 7. Financial invariants

Peylora must enforce:

- Provider confirmation cannot create Settlement.
- Settlement evidence cannot create or rewrite a Provider Transaction.
- Settlement recognition cannot increase received amounts or allocations.
- One settlement line can match at most one Provider Transaction unless the source schema establishes a legitimate one-to-many allocation and it is explicitly modelled.
- One Provider Transaction can match at most one source settlement line for one settlement source, unless the source explains a split adjustment.
- Idempotent import/reprocessing cannot duplicate batches, lines, matches or exceptions.
- Gross, fee and net calculations must remain source-defined and numerically exact.
- A bank deposit cannot be inferred from settlement status alone.
- Partial settlement and delayed payout must remain unresolved or partially matched, not fully reconciled.
- Reversals/adjustments must be append-only and must not rewrite the original settlement.
- Conflicts must remain visible until explicit operator resolution.
- Operator resolution must record actor, reason, timestamp, evidence and affected records.
- Merchant credit and invoice allocation remain driven by successful Provider Transactions, not settlement availability.
- Refund, reversal and payout correction policies require separate approved domain rules.

## 8. Security and merchant isolation

All settlement tables and evidence objects require a merchant foreign key or composite foreign key:

- Settlement import belongs to a Merchant.
- Batch, line, adjustment and deposit belong to the Merchant.
- Match and exception inherit the source Merchant.
- Evidence object key contains a merchant scope and import ID.
- Audit and resolution actions include actor and merchant scope where applicable.

Rules:

- Merchant A cannot read or resolve Merchant B's evidence.
- Merchant B cannot supply a bank reference that matches Merchant A's batch merely by matching a text field.
- ADMIN access is explicit and audited.
- Public endpoints never accept settlement evidence.
- Cross-merchant file object references are rejected.
- Merchant-facing status queries return only that Merchant's records.

## 9. Operational workflows

### Unmatched settlement

Create an exception and retain the settlement line. It remains visible while the Merchant or operator searches provider history.

### Missing transaction

Do not create a fabricated Provider Transaction. Investigate provider source evidence and keep the exception open.

### Amount discrepancy

Retain gross/net/provider amounts separately. Do not silently adjust received money or allocations.

### Unexpected fee

Create a fee/deduction record and exception if the source cannot explain it.

### Partial settlement

Record source line and settled amount. Match the settled portion; retain the remainder and delay status separately.

### Duplicate settlement record

Identical source fingerprint/natural key is idempotent. Materially different data creates a duplicate-exception and is not merged silently.

### Delayed payout

Record expected/deposit date where source provides it. TIMEOUT or delay must not convert a provider success to settlement success.

### Evidence conflict

Never overwrite one source with another. Preserve both and require explicit resolution.

### Operator resolution

Resolution may confirm or reject a match, but it must not:

- Fabricate provider evidence.
- Change Provider Transaction outcome.
- Mutate allocations or customer credit.
- Close evidence conflicts silently.

## 12. Proposed ADRs

### ADR-006: Settlement Evidence and Merchant Settlement Models

Decide settlement/batch/line/deposit terminology, source authority, effective dates and merchant bank account scope.

### ADR-007: Settlement Matching and Evidence Confidence

Define exact, candidate and operator-confirmed matches; duplicate source handling; ambiguity and discrepancy policies.

### ADR-008: Settlement Import and Source Profiles

Define supported CSV/Excel/PDF/API sources, schema validation, fingerprints, idempotency and raw-evidence retention.

### ADR-009: Fees, Deductions, Adjustments and Reversals

Define gross/net formulas, fee evidence, adjustments and whether provider reversals are actual refund states or separate settlement corrections.

### ADR-010: Settlement Exception Resolution

Define merchant/operator permissions, resolution states, audit requirements and whether a resolution can affect financial ledgers.

## 13. Potential implementation sequence and dependencies

1. **Approve settlement terminology and source authority**
   - Add ADR-006.
   - Identify the merchant account and bank deposit source that is authoritative.
2. **Create immutable evidence and source-profile foundation**
   - Add merchant-scoped import/evidence tables and object storage.
   - Start with one strict CSV profile.
3. **Add settlement batch, line and adjustment records**
   - Preserve raw fields and source row identity.
   - Define net/fee/amount formulas from the profile.
4. **Implement exact matching**
   - Match provider reference/receipt first.
   - Add candidate matching only after ambiguity rules are documented.
5. **Add exception and review workflows**
   - Use existing ADMIN authorization and audit patterns.
   - Keep resolution separate from accounting mutation.
6. **Add bank deposit and batch aggregation**
   - Reconcile net payout to deposit reference without inferring provider line settlement.
7. **Add future provider/API adapter**
   - Only after a documented official Safaricom settlement API is approved.

Dependencies:

```text
source authority + profile
  -> evidence storage
  -> canonical settlement data
  -> exact matching
  -> exception workflows
  -> batch/deposit reconciliation
  -> operator/manual and future API resolution
```

## 15. Risks, assumptions and unresolved decisions

### Risks

- Safaricom settlement reports may not expose a stable public API.
- CSV/Excel formats may change without notices.
- Provider and bank dates may differ from settlement-effective dates.
- Fees/deductions may be incomplete or aggregated.
- Bank deposits may aggregate multiple provider batches.
- Reversals may appear in transactions, settlement batches or both.
- OCR and PDF statements can introduce false matches.
- A real provider refund may appear as a negative adjustment and should not be conflated with allocation reversal.

### Assumptions

- Kenyan merchants receive at least one merchant-facing report or statement source, but exact source is not documented here.
- CSV/Excel import is the safest initial design target.
- PostgreSQL can remain the evidence and reconciliation store.
- Existing append-only reconciliation and audit patterns can be reused.

### Unknowns requiring maintainer decisions

- Is v0.3.0 reconciling settlement evidence, actual refunds, or both?
- Which Safaricom merchant evidence source(s) are in scope and what formats do merchants actually receive?
- Are settlement identifiers stable and authoritative for Paybill, Till and STK Push flows?
- How is settlement status represented: due, settled, paid, reversed, partial or bank-delayed?
- Which date is authoritative: transaction date, settlement date, value date or bank deposit date?
- Are gross, fees and net available per transaction or only at batch level?
- Does a settlement batch contain reversals/adjustments in the same row, a separate section or a separate report?
- How should a provider or banking record be correlated to a bank deposit?
- What should happen to a settlement discrepancy after operator review?
- May an authorised operator adjust accounting, or may settlement reconciliation only record discrepancies?
- What is the evidence-retention period and export deletion policy?
