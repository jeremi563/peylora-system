# Glossary

## Merchant

A Kenyan business that uses Peylora to collect, manage, monitor, and reconcile customer payments.

## Digital Payments and Collections Platform

A merchant-focused product that brings together payment collection and the operational context needed to understand, manage, and reconcile those payments.

## Payment Collection

The process of requesting or receiving money from a customer through an accepted payment channel.

## Payment Request

A merchant's request for a customer to pay a specified amount.

## Payment Link

A shareable interface through which a customer can access and fulfil a Payment Request.

## Invoice

A formal billing document associated with a Payment Request. It identifies an amount, customer, due date, and outstanding payment state.

## Payment Attempt

An attempt to collect a Payment Request through a payment provider. M-Pesa STK Push is the initial provider mechanism.

## Payment Transaction

The recorded provider outcome of a Payment Attempt, including provider references and confirmation details.

## Reconciliation

The process of matching confirmed payment outcomes to the merchant's customer, invoice, order, or other internal reference.

## Peylora

The product name. Peylora is a merchant-focused digital payments and collections platform for Kenyan businesses, with M-Pesa as the initial primary payment channel.
